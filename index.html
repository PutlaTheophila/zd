Integrated Logistics & Infrastructure Solutions

For over 39 years, Shri Dinesh Group has powered Indian industry with reliable transportation, heavy lifting, warehousing and — through our Zero Drive initiative — clean EV logistics. From a single truck to a 150+ owned fleet, 130+ cranes and 100+ acres of warehousing, our promise stays the same: Powering Industry. Moving India.

Our Services

Full Truck Load

Dedicated FTL movement across India with a strong, reliable fleet of 150+ owned trucks and trailers — backed by GPS tracking, ABS-equipped vehicles and experienced operations teams for on-time, secure delivery.

ODC & Over-Dimensional Movement

Specialised handling of over-dimensional and project cargo, supported by a dedicated pan-India cargo-securing team and route planning that keeps oversized consignments moving safely.

Clean EV Logistics — Zero Drive

Next-generation electric freight that cuts emissions without cutting reliability. Zero Drive replaces diesel haulage with connected EV trucks, helping our partners meet sustainability goals across the supply chain.

Crane & Heavy Lifting

Professional crane rental and heavy-lifting solutions up to 750 MTS, including next-gen EV cranes — engineered for demanding industrial and infrastructure projects with a modern, well-maintained fleet.

Warehousing & Supply Chain

100+ acres of strategic warehousing, including a 4-lakh-sq-ft facility at Bhiwandi, designed to support storage, distribution and end-to-end supply-chain management across the country.

Our Capabilities

Safety & Technology First

Every vehicle is equipped with GPS tracking and ABS, backed by safety-focused driver training and an in-house maintenance workshop. With 90% of our fleet under 8 years old, reliability is built into every kilometre we run.

Scale & Dedicated Teams

150+ owned vehicles and 350+ on long lease, managed by dedicated fleet-operations and cargo-securing teams. From single loads to nationwide programmes, we scale to your operation without losing control of quality.
